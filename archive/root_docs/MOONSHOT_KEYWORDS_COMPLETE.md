## ⚠️ **IMPORTANT WARNINGS**

### **1. Moonshots Are RARE**
- You might only find **1-2 true moonshots per month**
- Don't force trades - wait for genuine catalysts
- Most news is NOT moonshot material

### **2. High Risk, High Reward**
- Moonshots can move **+200-1000%** OR **-50-90%**
- Always use **smaller position sizes** (2-5% max)
- Set **stop losses** to protect capital

### **3. Timing Is Critical**
- Biggest moves happen **before** mainstream media
- Monitor **primary sources**: SEC filings, clinical trial databases, patent offices
- Speed matters - moonshots move fast

### **4. Fundamental Understanding Required**
- Don't trade keywords blindly
- Understand **WHY** the catalyst matters
- Research the company/sector before trading
